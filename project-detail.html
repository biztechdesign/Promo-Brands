Recycled Aluminium Ballpen

Product Tags

Ballpen

The Brief

Our client — a national retail chain with sustainability at the heart of their 2025 corporate strategy — needed 5,000 branded ballpens for an employee appreciation campaign. The hard requirement: zero single-use plastic.

Our Approach

We proposed the Recycled Aluminium Ballpen from our Eco range — made from post-consumer recycled aluminium with a premium weight and smooth, consistent write. Pens were packed into custom-printed FSC-certified kraft sleeves, then bulk-packed in recycled corrugated shippers.

The Result

All 5,000 units despatched within 12 working days — three days ahead of the client’s deadline. The client reported a 94% reduction in plastic versus their previous campaign.
