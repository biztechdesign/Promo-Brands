Moleskine

Moleskine is the legendary name behind the iconic black notebook — a timeless companion for artists, writers and thinkers since 1997. Combining Italian craftsmanship with functional simplicity, Moleskine products project an image of quality and creativity that resonates across every industry.

From hardcover notebooks to premium pens and messenger bags, Moleskine’s range pairs beautifully with corporate gifting programmes, employee welcome kits and high-end event merchandise. Add your brand to a product your recipients will keep and reach for every day.
